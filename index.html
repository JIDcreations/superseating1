Twenty-five years of fitting wheelchairs. Now I teach what I learned.

I’m Bart Van der Heyden, a physical therapist from Belgium. I train clinicians and care teams, and I help manufacturers and distributors get seating, mobility and wound care right.

A good seat isn’t furniture. It’s how someone gets through the day.

Posture, pressure, energy, independence: it all starts with how a person sits. I’ve spent my career on getting that right, and on helping others do the same.
