Clinical & regulatory support

Help with determining the best clinical, regulatory, marketing and communication strategy for Europe, Asia and the Middle East.

Discuss your project Read more

Strategy grounded in how devices are used

Bringing a seating, mobility or wound care device to a new market takes more than paperwork. It takes a clear view of who benefits, how clinicians will assess it, and how to explain it to them.

Clinical
Clinical applications and functionality of your device, the user groups it serves, and how it fits into assessment practice.
Regulatory
Input on your approach to the EU medical device market and to markets in Asia and the Middle East.
Marketing
Positioning that makes sense to prescribers, therapists and end users, based on clinical reality.
Communication
Clinical training, presentations and materials, delivered in English, Dutch, French or German.

Advice from someone who fits wheelchairs

Bart is a clinical consultant on the clinical application, design and functionality of medical devices for several manufacturers. He still assesses wheelchair users himself, so his advice reflects what happens in the clinic, not only what is on the spec sheet.
