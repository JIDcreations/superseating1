What this covers

Relationships built on clinical credibility

Prescribers and end users trust people who understand their work. Bart helps your organisation build and keep those relationships in Europe, the US, Asia and the Middle East.

Prescribers

Clinical education and product sessions for the therapists and specialists who specify seating and mobility equipment.

End users

Assessment and fitting know-how that helps equipment work for the people who use it every day.

Distribution teams

Training for your account managers and dealers, so they can talk to clinicians with confidence.

International

Experience in Europe, the US and Asia, and sessions in four languages.

How it works

Starting a collaboration

  1. Share your situation

    Your products, markets and the prescriber or user relationships you want to strengthen.

  2. Agree on an approach

    Training, visits, events or ongoing support: a plan that fits your region and team.

  3. Work together

    Hands-on support alongside your team, in the language your audience speaks.
