Product development

From user needs to design changes

Support at every stage of developing products for wound care, seating and mobility, with the end user and the prescribing clinician in mind throughout.

  1. Determine needs

    Identify unmet needs in the wound care, seating and mobility field, from users, therapists and care teams.

  2. Generate and screen ideas

    Set up focus groups, run idea generation and screen concepts against clinical requirements.

  3. Prototype and evaluate

    Prototyping, evaluation and improvement of concepts with real users in real settings.

  4. Implement design changes

    Turn evaluation findings into concrete, clinically sound design changes.

Grounded in practice

Designed with the people who use it

Bart assesses many wheelchair users and looks for practical solutions to specific seating, mobility and wound care problems. That daily contact with users feeds directly into product development work.

User-centred

Focus groups and evaluations with wheelchair users, therapists and care staff.

Clinically informed

Biomechanics, positioning and pressure management built into the brief from day one.
