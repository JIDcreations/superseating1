Background

Clinical practice, teaching and product know-how

Bart is licensed and has practised as a physical therapist in Belgium, Germany and the USA. Now based in Belgium, he travels all over the world for lectures, educational events and hands-on sessions for varied audiences. He covers seating and mobility issues, wheelchair provision for children and adults with moderate to severe physical challenges, and wound care.

He keeps a strong hands-on practice and helps out at De Kine, his wife’s private physical therapy practice. There he assesses many wheelchair users and looks for practical, often innovative, solutions to specific seating, mobility and wound care problems.

Bart is also a clinical consultant on the clinical application, design and functionality of medical devices for several manufacturers.
